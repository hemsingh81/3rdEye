How it works

Three things that actually matter to a parent — not a technical feature list.

Real screen-time lockout

Set a daily limit or bedtime, and the device actually locks at that moment — not just a report you read the next day.

Content filtering that just works

Block adult, violent, and gambling content by category, plus your own custom list — filtered on the device, instantly.

App control, not just app visibility

Nothing installs without your approval. If something gets on the device anyway, we block it from running — and you can remove it remotely.

Nothing hidden. From anyone.

This is the exact same list your child sees in their own "what my parent can see" screen — we don't say one thing to you and show another to them.

What we track

  • ✓ Screen time and app usage
  • ✓ Location and arrival/departure alerts
  • ✓ App install requests and installed apps
  • ✓ Web category (not full browsing history)

What we never track

  • ✗ Message or call content
  • ✗ Microphone or camera, ever
  • ✗ Full browsing history at the URL level
  • ✗ Anything not shown on your child's own screen
The 3rdEye icon is always visible on the device — it cannot be hidden, by us or by anyone.
